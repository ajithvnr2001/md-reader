# Photosynthesis — Quick Notes

Photosynthesis is the process plants use to convert **light energy** into
**chemical energy** stored in glucose.

## The equation

6CO2 + 6H2O + light energy -> C6H12O6 + 6O2

## Key stages

1. **Light-dependent reactions** — happen in the thylakoid membrane;
   produce ATP, NADPH, and oxygen.
2. **Light-independent reactions (Calvin cycle)** — happen in the
   stroma; use ATP and NADPH to build glucose from CO2.

## Why it matters

- Produces the oxygen we breathe
- Forms the base of almost every food chain on Earth
- Removes CO2 from the atmosphere, influencing climate

## Common exam mistakes

- Confusing chlorophyll (the pigment) with chloroplast (the organelle)
- Forgetting that oxygen released comes from water, not CO2
- Mixing up the Calvin cycle with cellular respiration (they are opposites)
